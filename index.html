02 / PROJECTFLOW CASE STUDY

From scattered project updates to a clear delivery view.

ProjectFlow demonstrates a structured approach to project visibility: identifying delivery information that matters, turning it into functional requirements, and reflecting it in a working dashboard.

Problem Delivery status, ownership and timelines can be difficult to follow when updates are scattered.
Stakeholders Business users, approvers, technology teams, QA/UAT participants and project owners.
Requirements Track project details, lifecycle stage, priority, deadline, owner and status in one place.
User stories Create records, monitor status and protect forward lifecycle progression.
Acceptance criteria Required fields, valid deadline, status filtering and no backward stage movement.
Outcome A working delivery view designed for clearer follow-up and more consistent project tracking.
